[2026-03-06] Analyzed cross-entropy-loss (sk_034). Found 5 optimization opportunities (medium potential). Key findings: Forward (3-pass log-sum-exp) and backward (softmax gradient) kernels for training loss. Both use nd_range<1> with WG=WARP_SIZE=16 — only 1 sub-group per work-group, severely under-utilizing hardware (recommended 256). Uses correct reqd_sub_group_size(WARP_SIZE) and warp_reduce_max/warp_reduce_sum for sub-group reductions. SLM caching of logits when nclasses*4 <= 48KB (well-designed). CRITICAL: WG=16 means only 16 threads process each row — with typical vocabulary sizes (32K-65K), each thread loops 2000-4000 times. Increasing to WG=256 with block_reduce would reduce per-thread iterations by 16x. No vectorized loads (scalar float per iteration). Uses sycl::exp/sycl::log instead of native variants. No #pragma unroll on hot inner loops. Forward pass has extra reduce_rows_f32_sycl kernel launch for summing per-row losses — could be fused with atomicAdd. Training-only kernel, not on inference critical path. ~35-45% estimated efficiency.

Compute-bound kernel dominated by exp() calls: Mamba-1 calls sycl::exp 16 times per time step per thread in hot inner loop; sycl::native::exp could be 2-4x faster.
